The Weekly Tape

The written notes — one market, one week, read across season, the hedging program and term structure. The automated COT releases run in the Hedgers’ Ledger.

Each note takes one market and reads it against three signals: the season, the commercial hedging program from the CFTC's Commitments of Traders report, and the term structure of the futures curve. A market showing two of the three is left alone, which is why some weeks cover grains and some cover nothing but rates. The three-signal read sets out the method, and what it deliberately will not do.
