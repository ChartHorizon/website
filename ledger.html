The Hedgers’ Ledger

The weekly release, in two parts: the markets where the commercial book sits at the edge of its own three-year range, and then the hedging program — where every market’s net sits inside its own trailing six-month range. Both straight from the CFTC report. The written notes run in The Weekly Tape.
