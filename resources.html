ONE LINK PER ITEM, the headline. A second "watch it here" link under the same headline would be two names for one destination, which reads as two places to anyone tabbing or listening through the page — the same rule the download line on /dashboard/ follows. PAID LINKS: `affiliate: true` on a section marks every item in it; `featured: true` additionally sets it as a plate. The two are separate flags on purpose — a section can be paid without being promoted, and the disclosure must not depend on the decoration. Two things follow and neither is decoration. The chip prints AT the link, because § 5a Abs. 4 UWG wants the commercial purpose recognisable there and not only in the note above; and rel gains `sponsored nofollow`, which is Google's requirement for a paid link. The chip is deliberately NOT gold: gold is the slot the desk's own signal sits in, and a disclosure that borrows the accent colour reads as a recommendation. {%- endcomment -%}
