The report dated 2026-09-22 puts eleven markets at a three-year positioning extreme: seven at lows, four at highs. Three of them set fresh window records, Lean Hogs and Class III Milk on the high side and the 5-Year U.S. T-Note on the low, where the commercial book is still net long but the least long it has been in three years. Four markets are new to the list: Coffee and the British Pound at highs, the E-mini Nasdaq 100 and Copper at lows. Four left it since last week: Wheat, the 2-Year T-Note, the 30-Year T-Bond and Bitcoin. The British Pound is the one market in both halves of this issue, a new three-year extreme and one of the five programs below that crossed their six-month midpoint with this report.

## The hedging program

Where each market's tracked COT net sits inside its own trailing six-month range: above the midpoint of that range or below it. This is the same positioning read at close quarters, and it answers a different question from the extremes above: a book can sit at a three-year low and still have been working higher for six months. What follows is only what MOVED: the markets that crossed that midpoint with this week's report.

20 programs read above the midpoint of their own trailing six-month range this week, 19 below. Five crossed it with this report: Soybean Oil from below to above, Cocoa from below to above, British Pound from below to above, Swiss Franc from above to below and Ethereum from above to below.
