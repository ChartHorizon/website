The 18 August 2026 commitments show nine markets registering a positioning extreme against the three-year range — four at the high, five at the low — with the short side carrying the heavier freight. Sugar #11 and Cotton #2 each sit at a fresh window record on the low side, though only Sugar is new to the ledger; Cotton held the same corner in the previous report and has now dug it deeper. Four names enter this week — Sugar, Lean Hogs, the 30-Year T-Bond and Chicago Wheat — and one leaves conspicuously: the E-mini Nasdaq 100, a record hedger high at +17,475 contracts a week ago, has swung to 12,347 net short and is off the board altogether. When both ends of the government curve stand at hedger highs while two softs sit at record lows, the tape is rarely whispering.
