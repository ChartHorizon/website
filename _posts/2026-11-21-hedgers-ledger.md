The report dated 2026-11-17 puts thirteen markets at a three-year positioning extreme, all thirteen at highs, up from ten a week ago. Five set fresh window records with this report. Bitcoin, the Australian Dollar and Lean Hogs extend the records they already held a week ago; Bitcoin's tracked book is the leveraged funds, not the commercials, and the Australian Dollar's commercials added another 35,856 contracts net in the week. Gold and Platinum set new ones from the 98th and the 99th percentiles: Gold's commercials covered 6,290 contracts of shorts and are now 4,591 net short. Silver gives up last week's record and holds at the 98th percentile. Three markets are new to the list: WTI Crude Oil and the New Zealand Dollar, both just under the 95th-percentile cutoff a week ago, and Live Cattle, up from the 92nd. None of last week's ten left it.

## The hedging program

Where each market's tracked COT net sits inside its own trailing six-month range: above the midpoint of that range or below it. This is the same positioning read at close quarters, and it answers a different question from the extremes above: a book can sit at a three-year low and still have been working higher for six months. What follows is only what MOVED: the markets that crossed that midpoint with this week's report.

27 programs read above the midpoint of their own trailing six-month range this week, 12 below. Two crossed it with this report: E-mini S&P 500 from above to below and E-mini Dow ($5) from below to above.
