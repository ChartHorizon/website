The report dated 2026-10-20 puts fourteen markets at a three-year positioning extreme: ten at highs, four at lows. Three set fresh window records with this report, Bitcoin and Platinum on the high side and Sugar #11 on the low. Platinum and Sugar #11 extend records they already held a week ago; Bitcoin, at the 99th percentile last week, reaches its first, though there the tracked book is the leveraged funds, not the commercials. Euro FX, Lean Hogs and Class III Milk, all records a week ago, stay on the board just short of them, at the 99th, 98th and 98th percentiles. Four markets are new to the list: the New Zealand Dollar at a high (83rd percentile a week ago), and the E-mini Nasdaq 100, the 5-Year U.S. T-Note and Brent Crude Oil at lows, the Nasdaq 100 and Brent back after a single week off it. None left, so where last week's board had one low, this one has four.

## The hedging program

Where each market's tracked COT net sits inside its own trailing six-month range: above the midpoint of that range or below it. This is the same positioning read at close quarters, and it answers a different question from the extremes above: a book can sit at a three-year low and still have been working higher for six months. What follows is only what MOVED: the markets that crossed that midpoint with this week's report.

25 programs read above the midpoint of their own trailing six-month range this week, 14 below. Eight crossed it with this report: RBOB Gasoline from below to above, Corn from below to above, Wheat (Chicago SRW) from below to above, Coffee (Arabica) from above to below, E-mini Dow ($5) from below to above, E-mini Russell 2000 from above to below, Canadian Dollar from above to below and 30-Year U.S. T-Bond from above to below.
