The report dated 2026-11-03 puts thirteen markets at a three-year positioning extreme: eleven at highs, two at lows. Four set fresh window records with this report, all on the high side. Bitcoin, Euro FX and Platinum extend records they already held a week ago, though Bitcoin's tracked book is the leveraged funds, not the commercials. Gold, at the 99th percentile a week ago, sets a new one. Lean Hogs give up the record they set a week ago and sit at the 99th percentile; the commercials are still net long, by 447 contracts. Silver and the E-mini Nasdaq 100 are new to the list, Silver at the 97th percentile after the 94th a week ago, the Nasdaq at the 3rd after the 5th. The 5-Year U.S. T-Note left it: its commercials added 229,097 contracts net in the week, which lifts the book from the 4th percentile to the 22nd.

## The hedging program

Where each market's tracked COT net sits inside its own trailing six-month range: above the midpoint of that range or below it. This is the same positioning read at close quarters, and it answers a different question from the extremes above: a book can sit at a three-year low and still have been working higher for six months. What follows is only what MOVED: the markets that crossed that midpoint with this week's report.

26 programs read above the midpoint of their own trailing six-month range this week, 13 below. Seven crossed it with this report: Brent Crude Oil from above to below, Soybeans from below to above, Coffee (Arabica) from below to above, Feeder Cattle from below to above, E-mini Dow ($5) from above to below, British Pound from above to below and 5-Year U.S. T-Note from below to above. Two of them reverse last week's turns: Brent Crude Oil is back below the midpoint and Feeder Cattle back above it.
