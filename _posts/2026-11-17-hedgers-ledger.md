The report dated 2026-11-10 puts ten markets at a three-year positioning extreme, all ten at highs. Four set fresh window records with this report. Bitcoin extends the record it already held a week ago, though its tracked book is the leveraged funds, not the commercials. The Australian Dollar, at the 96th percentile a week ago, sets a new one: its commercials added 25,968 contracts net in the week. Lean Hogs and Silver set theirs from the 99th and the 97th. Platinum, Gold and Euro FX give up last week's records and hold at the 99th, 98th and 97th percentiles. Natural Gas is new to the list: its commercials turned net long, by 14,748 contracts, after 418 net short a week ago. Four markets left it: the New Zealand Dollar and WTI Crude Oil, both now just under the 95th-percentile cutoff, and the E-mini Nasdaq 100 and Sugar #11, last week's only two lows.

## The hedging program

Where each market's tracked COT net sits inside its own trailing six-month range: above the midpoint of that range or below it. This is the same positioning read at close quarters, and it answers a different question from the extremes above: a book can sit at a three-year low and still have been working higher for six months. What follows is only what MOVED: the markets that crossed that midpoint with this week's report.

27 programs read above the midpoint of their own trailing six-month range this week, 12 below. One crossed it with this report: Cocoa, from below the midpoint to above it.
