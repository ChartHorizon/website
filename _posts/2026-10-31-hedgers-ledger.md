The report dated 2026-10-27 puts twelve markets at a three-year positioning extreme: ten at highs, two at lows. Four set fresh window records with this report, all on the high side. Bitcoin and Platinum extend records they already held a week ago, though Bitcoin's tracked book is the leveraged funds, not the commercials. Euro FX and Lean Hogs, at the 99th and 98th percentiles a week ago, set new ones, and the Lean Hogs commercials are net long for the first time in the window, by 1,069 contracts. Sugar #11 gives up the record it held a week ago and sits at the 3rd percentile. WTI Crude Oil is the only market new to the list, at the 96th percentile after the 94th a week ago. The E-mini Nasdaq 100, the British Pound and Brent Crude Oil left it, so where last week's board had four lows, this one has two.

## The hedging program

Where each market's tracked COT net sits inside its own trailing six-month range: above the midpoint of that range or below it. This is the same positioning read at close quarters, and it answers a different question from the extremes above: a book can sit at a three-year low and still have been working higher for six months. What follows is only what MOVED: the markets that crossed that midpoint with this week's report.

25 programs read above the midpoint of their own trailing six-month range this week, 14 below. Two crossed it with this report: Brent Crude Oil from below to above and Feeder Cattle from above to below.
